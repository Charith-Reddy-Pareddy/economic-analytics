THE PLATFORM

From public data to decision-ready insight.

Reproducible analysis

Cleaning, feature engineering, correlation analysis, and anomaly screening are implemented in testable Python modules.

RESEARCH USE

Designed for transparent empirical work.

Every observation retains its indicator, country, unit, date, and source. The platform supports descriptive research and hypothesis generation while clearly distinguishing correlation from causal evidence.

  • Documented missing-data handling

  • Exportable analytical subsets
